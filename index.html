How the first-order offer works

A customer is new when their phone number has no earlier entry. Their first order or ride gets the discount below.

Town Fetch: the discount is taken off the service charge. Items are billed at the actual price, and the medicine extra is not discounted.

Bike Taxi: the discount is taken off one way of the fare. Waiting time and the return leg are charged in full.

Google Sheet connection

One-time setup (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open your Google Sheet. Click Extensions then Apps Script.
  2. Delete everything in the editor and paste the whole Code.gs file.
  3. On line 13 replace CHANGE-THIS-PIN with your own PIN. Click the save icon.
  4. Click Deploy, then New deployment. Click the gear and choose Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  6. Click Authorize access, choose your Google account. If Google says the app is not verified, click Advanced then Go to project and Allow.
  7. Copy the Web app URL. Paste it above with your PIN and click Save and test connection.

Your partner does the last step only, on their own phone or computer, using the same link and PIN. Entries, customers and rates are stored in three tabs of your sheet: Entries, Customers and Rates. Do not rename or reorder the first-row headings. If you edit the script later, use Deploy then Manage deployments then Edit then New version.
